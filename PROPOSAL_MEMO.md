# Presentation proposal

Status: unofficial demonstration with synthetic member data. This document replaces the earlier production and strategic proposal for the scope of this repository.

## Purpose

Make an alternative public-site and member-workspace experience concrete enough to review. The demo lets reviewers navigate representative screens and try local simulations without an internal account or connection to existing infrastructure.

## What reviewers can assess

- Whether navigation makes the intended services easy to find.
- Whether dues, events, documents and participation flows are understandable.
- Whether empty, failed, pending and confirmation states communicate the next step.
- Whether fictional member and administrator views explain the proposed differences.
- Whether the layout remains usable on desktop and mobile.

## Boundaries

The member fixtures are fictional. Payments, responses and settings changes are local simulations, not operational records. Role switching is a presentation control, not authentication or authorization.

Public pages include explanatory proposals and external references. Their presence does not establish an integration, an official position, or a verified current dataset.

Access to an existing internal service was provided for observation and learning only. It does not authorize copying private records into the demo or connecting this repository to that service.

## Review outcome

Feedback should identify a specific screen, the confusing or missing behavior, and the proposed improvement. Accepted changes remain within the standalone demo unless the user explicitly defines a separate scope.

Current features and commands are documented in [README.md](README.md). Maintenance steps are in [IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md](IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md), and member-workspace details are in [MEMBER_AREA_PLAN.md](MEMBER_AREA_PLAN.md).
