# Proposal memo: USR public digital platform

Status: unofficial draft. The prototype uses synthetic data and must not be presented as an approved USR product.

## Executive thesis

USR should treat the public site as civic infrastructure, not only as an editorial channel. The current public site already has recognizable entry points: news, people, government, organization, contact, joining and donation. The proposal keeps that structure, but adds the missing operating layer beneath it: every important claim, person, local fact, financial item or public-interest dossier has source, owner, verified date, limitation, status, permalink and next action.

This matters because a party stuck around low double-digit or single-digit support across election types cannot rely only on campaign peaks, national media access or generic anti-corruption language. It needs a durable public record that gives voters, journalists, members, donors and local branches something verifiable to use every week.

## Why this makes sense now

The political problem is not only message quality. It is distribution, trust and local repeatability.

When the media environment is difficult, an article or press release disappears quickly. A sourced dossier can keep producing value: a journalist can verify it, a branch can localize it, an elected official can use it in council or Parliament, a member can ask a precise question, and a donor can understand what digital capacity pays for.

When corruption or public-money topics are framed only as accusations, opponents can answer with noise: denial, counter-attack, legal intimidation or procedural confusion. When the platform frames a case as `indicator de risc`, document, chronology, method, right to reply and next institutional question, the opponent is pushed onto factual terrain.

## Strategic advantage versus opponents

The advantage is not automation or louder posting. The advantage is a better evidence machine.

1. **Harder to dismiss**
   A claim with document, date, method, limitation and right of reply is harder to dismiss than a slogan.

2. **Reusable by many actors**
   The same record can serve the public site, press room, local branch, elected official, member task and donor narrative.

3. **Local by default**
   National positions become useful when connected to county, UAT, HCL, budget line, person and next local action.

4. **Credibility through visible gaps**
   Missing CVs, missing answers, late budgets and incomplete HCL indexes should be visible. Showing gaps builds more trust than hiding them.

5. **Owned channel under media blockade**
   The site becomes a public archive and distribution base. Media coverage becomes useful, but not required for every fact to exist.

6. **Lower legal and reputational risk**
   Careful statuses reduce reckless accusation risk: `indicator`, `documentat`, `răspuns cerut`, `răspuns primit`, `rectificat`, `închis`.

## How the party extracts value

### For voters

The voter can answer simple questions quickly:

- Who represents me?
- What did they vote?
- What is missing from their public record?
- What does the party propose on this issue?
- What source supports this number?
- What can I attend, sign, ask or verify locally?

This changes the site from passive reading to public utility.

### For members

Members need verbs, not just news:

- verify a source;
- prepare a local meeting;
- document a HCL vote;
- assemble a field pack;
- ask for a missing CV;
- route a local issue to the right elected official.

The member area should not auto-post. It should help people do better manual political work.

### For local branches

Branches get a local operating board:

- data gaps by UAT;
- reusable explanations;
- local budget and HCL indicators;
- press-ready charts;
- clear owners for repair work;
- meeting questions connected to public records.

This is especially important outside national campaigns, where weak local cadence keeps the party invisible.

### For press

The press room should provide more than contact details:

- chronology;
- primary document;
- chart;
- CSV;
- method note;
- limitation;
- contact by domain;
- right-of-reply status.

This lowers the cost for journalists to cover a story and makes the party more useful even when editorial space is hostile or limited.

### For donors

Donation should be connected to capacity:

- data products;
- verified sources;
- local packs;
- member workflows;
- press assets;
- transparency about subvention and branch support.

The message is: money builds infrastructure that keeps working after one ad cycle.

## Anti-corruption framing

The platform should avoid careless claims. The discipline should be:

1. Start with the public document or missing public document.
2. State the method and limitation.
3. Use cautious status language.
4. Offer right of reply.
5. Publish answers fully or mark non-answer visibly.
6. Convert the case into concrete institutional questions.
7. Let branches and elected officials reuse the record.

This approach supports anti-corruption politics without turning the site into a defamation risk engine.

## The low-percentage problem

Low national, local and European vote shares should be treated as a product and organization problem, not only a communication problem.

The party needs:

- more frequent contact with useful facts;
- clearer proof of competence;
- local relevance outside campaign windows;
- member work that compounds;
- donor confidence that money creates durable assets;
- press materials that reduce verification friction.

A record-backed platform helps because each piece of work can be reused. One sourced local budget indicator can become a public page, a press chart, a council question, a member task, a donor proof point and a meeting agenda item.

## Pilot recommendation

Do not build the full platform first. Run three pilots.

### Pilot 1: one county

Pick one county with enough public data and an active branch. Publish a limited local board:

- UAT completeness;
- HCL index sample;
- budget execution indicators;
- missing-data list;
- branch owner for each repair.

### Pilot 2: one person record

Build one real elected-official record:

- public role;
- CV state;
- public contact;
- votes;
- projects;
- source links;
- missing fields;
- correction path.

### Pilot 3: one public-interest dossier

Pick one corruption, procurement or public-money case where documentation exists. Publish:

- risk indicator;
- primary documents;
- chronology;
- method;
- right of reply;
- press pack;
- local member actions.

## Governance required before real launch

This cannot become real without ownership.

- Editorial owner for every public page type.
- Data owner for every source.
- Legal/GDPR reviewer for member and sensitive records.
- Correction policy.
- Right-of-reply policy.
- Visibility model: public, member, bureau.
- Audit log for sensitive access.
- No auto-posting or coordinated identical posting.

## Decision requested

Approve the prototype as a discussion artifact and authorize a narrow pilot brief:

- one county;
- one person record;
- one public-interest dossier;
- one press pack;
- one member workflow;
- one donor-facing transparency proof.

The success test is not whether the interface looks impressive. The test is whether a voter, journalist, member, local branch and donor can each extract a clear, verifiable next action from the same underlying records.
